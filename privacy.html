Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Front Armor is a game made by Koray Kural ("we"). This policy covers the Front Armor app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

The short version

Front Armor collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not send anything about you to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (gold, garage upgrades, blueprints, stars and unlocked tanks) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved on your device only. We cannot see them. They are deleted when you delete the app. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include them, under that service's terms.

Network

The game plays fully offline and does not connect to our servers or to third-party services.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions. It uses vibration for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's options.

Children

Front Armor does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: frontarmor-support@koraykural.com.
