Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Front Armor is a game made by Koray Kural ("we"). This policy covers the Front Armor app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

The short version

We never ask for your name, email or contacts. When you are online, the game gives you an anonymous player account with a public gamer tag for the leaderboards, through Unity Gaming Services. You can link it to Sign in with Apple or Google Play Games to keep your progress on other devices. The game also uses two Google services: Firebase Analytics, to see how the game is played, and Google AdMob, to show optional rewarded ads. These services collect device and usage data as described below.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (gold, medals, stars, unlocked tanks, garage upgrades, armory parts, missions and your purchase record) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved on your device. Unless you turn on "Save progress" (see below), the progress is not sent anywhere, and it is deleted when you delete the app. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include them, under that service's terms.

Player account and leaderboards (Unity Gaming Services)

When the game first goes online it signs you in anonymously with Unity Gaming Services (Unity Player Authentication). This creates a random player ID; no name, email or phone number is involved. The game picks a random gamer tag such as "IronBadger", which you can change in Settings. The following is stored with Unity and shown publicly on the in-game leaderboards:

  • your gamer tag;
  • your best clear time per level, with the tank, garage upgrade levels and upgrade parts you used and whether you used the ad revive;
  • your kills and gold earned for the daily, weekly and all-time boards, with the tank you used most.

Save progress (Sign in with Apple, Google Play Games)

If you choose "Save progress" in Settings, your player account is linked to Sign in with Apple (iPhone, iPad) or Google Play Games (Android). We receive an account identifier from Apple or Google, not your name or email. Your game progress is then copied to Unity Cloud Save so you can restore it after a reinstall or on another device.

Unity processes this data for us as described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, for example which levels are started and finished, which tank is used, how long a level takes, and whether a rewarded ad was watched. With these events Firebase collects an app-instance identifier, device model and operating system, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address (country or region), and session data. We use this only to improve the game. It is not used to identify you. See Firebase privacy.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads in Front Armor are optional: you choose to watch one to respawn after your tank is destroyed or to double the gold from a won level. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your IP address, device and app information, ad interactions and, on Android, the Advertising ID, to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. On iPhone and iPad the game does not ask to track you, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared.

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and where else the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings under "Privacy + ads".
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Medal packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play, which handle the payment; we never see your payment details. The game keeps a record of the purchases it has delivered (product and order ID) with your progress, so a purchase is not delivered twice and stays in your cloud save if you use "Save progress".

Offline play

The game plays fully offline. When you are offline, no analytics are sent, no ads are shown, and leaderboard scores wait on the device until you are back online.

Children

Front Armor is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete data about you.

  • Delete your account: in the game, open Settings and tap Delete account. This deletes your player account, gamer tag, leaderboard entries and saved progress. See Delete your account for other ways.
  • Analytics and ad data are not linked to your player account; Google processes them as described in its policies. You can stop all collection by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
