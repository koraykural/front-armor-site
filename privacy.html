Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Front Armor is a game made by Koray Kural ("we"). This policy covers the Front Armor app for iPhone, iPad and Android.

The short version

Front Armor has no account and we never ask for your name, email or contacts. The game uses two Google services: Firebase Analytics, to see how the game is played, and Google AdMob, to show optional rewarded ads. These services collect device and usage data as described below.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (gold, garage upgrades, blueprints, stars and unlocked tanks) and your settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved on your device only. We cannot see them. They are deleted when you delete the app. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google backup) may include them, under that service's terms.

Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, for example which levels are started and finished, which tank is used, how long a level takes, and whether a rewarded ad was watched. With these events Firebase collects an app-instance identifier, device model and operating system, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address (country or region), and session data. We use this only to improve the game. It is not used to identify you. See Firebase privacy.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads in Front Armor are optional: you choose to watch one to respawn after your tank is destroyed or to double the gold from a won level. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions, to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. If you allow it, ads may be personalized.

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and where else the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings under "Privacy choices".
  • iOS tracking: on iPhone and iPad the system asks whether the app may track you. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized based on it.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Offline play

The game plays fully offline. When you are offline, no analytics are sent until you are back online, and no ads are shown.

Children

Front Armor is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete data about you. Because we hold no account or contact details, we cannot link analytics or ad data to you personally; Google processes it as described in its policies. You can stop all collection by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
