Support

Need help with Front Armor, found a bug, or have an idea? Email frontarmor-support@koraykural.com. We usually reply within a few days. If you report a bug, the level, the tank and your phone model help a lot.

How to play

Drag anywhere to steer. Your tank always drives forward and the gun fires on its own at whatever is in range. Keep your thick front armor toward the enemy and use buildings, rocks and trees as cover.

Each level has a stronghold (the main goal) and support sites around it. Destroying a support site makes the stronghold weaker and earns you an upgrade pick. Tap Upgrade when you have a quiet moment to choose one. Some levels have other goals: defend an outpost, escort a train, raid an armored train, hunt a convoy or rescue captured crews.

Questions

Do I need an internet connection? No, the game plays offline.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. To keep it after a reinstall or on a new phone, open Settings and tap Save progress (Sign in with Apple on iPhone, Google Play Games on Android). Later, the same button restores it.

How do I change my gamer tag? Settings, then Change next to your gamer tag. Tags are 3 to 16 letters, numbers or underscores.

How do I delete my account? See Delete your account.

Do I have to watch ads? No. Ads are optional: watch one to respawn after your tank is destroyed, or to double the gold from a won level.

Are there in-app purchases? Yes, optional medal packs in the Shop. Medals are also earned by playing (first-time stars, daily missions, career ranks), and every level can be finished without buying anything. If a purchase does not arrive, email us with the date and the store receipt.

Which tank should I pick? Read the briefing. Scout is best against infantry and open ground, Heavy against enemy tanks, Brawler for a mix, Crusher for ramming through crowds. Any tank can finish any level; the right one is faster and earns the third star.
