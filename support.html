Support

Need help with Front Armor, found a bug, or have an idea? Email frontarmor-support@koraykural.com. We usually reply within a few days. If you report a bug, the level, the tank and your phone model help a lot.

How to play

Drag anywhere to steer. Your tank always drives forward and the gun fires on its own at whatever is in range. Keep your thick front armor toward the enemy and use buildings, rocks and trees as cover.

Each level has a stronghold (the main goal) and support sites around it. Destroying a support site makes the stronghold weaker and earns you an upgrade pick. Scrap from wrecks fills the XP bar for more picks. Tap Upgrade when you have a quiet moment to choose one.

Questions

Do I need an internet connection? No, the game plays offline.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Are there in-app purchases? No.

Which tank should I pick? Read the briefing. Scout is best against infantry and open ground, Heavy against enemy tanks, Brawler for a mix. Any tank can finish any level; the right one is faster and earns the third star.
