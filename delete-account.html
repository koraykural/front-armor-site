Delete your account

Front Armor by Koray Kural gives each player an online account for the leaderboards and, if you turn on "Save progress", for your saved progress. You can delete it at any time.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Front Armor and tap the gear button to open Settings.
  2. Find Delete account and tap Delete, then tap again to confirm.

The game needs an internet connection for this. Deletion happens right away.

By email

If you no longer have the game, email frontarmor-support@koraykural.com with your gamer tag (shown in Settings, for example IronBadger#1234). We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • your player account and its link to Sign in with Apple or Google Play Games;
  • your gamer tag;
  • your leaderboard entries;
  • your saved progress in the cloud.

Nothing is kept after deletion. Progress stored on your phone is reset too when you delete in the game; if you delete by email, deleting the app removes it. Analytics and ad data are not linked to your account; see the Privacy Policy.
